SCOPE
The grasp demo begins with the cube placed in the upward-facing palm. It tests
physical grip retention and lift, not perception or autonomous pickup from a table.
The five-axis arm uses position-only inverse kinematics; arbitrary 6D orientation
and collision-free motion planning are not implemented by that solver.
Motor torque limits and rigid-body masses are concept assumptions, not verified
hardware ratings. CAD is a concept, not a production release. No physical robot
has been manufactured, wired, calibrated, or tested.
