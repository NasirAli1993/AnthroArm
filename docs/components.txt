Base · M8 table mounting plate
  Robot link: base_link
  Anchors the arm to a rigid bench. Four 9 mm clearance holes on 180 mm PCD; rear service port; central cable passage. Anchoring, mass and bolt selection require structural validation.

Base · removable trim ring
  Robot link: base_link

  Robot link: waist_link

Palm · 100 × 84 × 32 mm shell

Palm · removable dorsal cover

Palm · compliant contact pad concept

DESIGN STATUS
This is a concept CAD assembly for on-screen simulation and engineering study.
The CAD and physics share frames and lengths; mass and collision primitives remain approximations.
Physical tendon actuation, component selection, structural loads, tolerances and safety need a separate validated hardware engineering phase.
