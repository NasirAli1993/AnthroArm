ASTER-25 ANATOMICAL - SIMULATION METHODS AND MEASURED RESULTS

Visuals: 101 exact CAD meshes in metres.
Contacts: 40 convex hulls of CAD shells/pads.
Visual meshes use group 1; hidden collision hulls use group 3. Floor/object use group 0.
Hulls can fill shell cavities and small concavities; this is not exact concave CAD collision.

Control: 25 position servos. A separate nominal reference follows joint speed limits.
Modeled gravity/Coriolis bias is fed through the same effort-limited actuators.
There are no applied external control torques and no body gravity cancellation.
Physical hinge stops, bounded targets, actuator torque limits and self-contact remain active.

Seven v2 precision/contact checks passed. All 25 axes moved independently.
Free-space steady joint error <0.01 deg; palm target error and return spread <0.01 mm.
These are idealized numerical model results, not physical hardware precision claims.
Contact intentionally prevents a finger from reaching a target inside another solid.
The isolated free-space spread check uses 0.025 rad; larger inward spread meets neighboring digits.

Seeded grasp: 60 g block lifted 293.17 mm.
Middle/ring contact retained for 100% of the 2 s hold window.
Block is placed in the upward palm only at initial reset. No teleport or weld is used afterward.
This validates grip retention and lift from a seeded grasp, not autonomous table pickup.

Thumb-index pinch uses the actual CAD distal pad collision surfaces and nonzero contact force.
The separate historical fingertip endpoint gap is a centreline-site distance, not the pad gap.
MuJoCo uses a combined 0.4 mm contact margin, allowing force at a small positive gap.
