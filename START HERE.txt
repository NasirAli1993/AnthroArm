SCOPE
These are bounded simulation experiments, not a fabricated robot or a general
commercial autonomy system. The learned policy is supervised regression for
known upright cubes; task stages and IK are scripted. Navigation uses custom
lidar ICP/A*, not Nav2 or visual SLAM. Combined docking uses an unobstructed
lane, heavy base, fixed external camera and ideal base pose for arm IK.
The local VLM recognized the cropped cube but misdescribed the full scene;
its output does not control motion. No general VLA policy is claimed.
Jetson hardware execution and sim-to-real transfer were not performed.
Isaac Sim/Lab were not run: this GTX 1660 Ti lacks the supported RTX hardware.
See integration\runtime\isaac_feasibility.txt for the official-source review.

LIMITS TO EXPLAIN HONESTLY
The full arm is a digital concept and physics model. Motors, cable shapes, inertias and effort ratings are engineering assumptions. Full-arm load-bearing structure, drives, harness, thermal performance and stopping system are not fabricated or validated.
The starter finger has passed CAD topology/export and limited-angle interference checks, but has not been printed, fitted to a purchased servo or load-tested.
Simulation precision is not physical accuracy. RGB-D autonomy assumes the known upright orange 50 mm cube, calibrated fixed camera and worktable. It does not demonstrate arbitrary-object recognition or autonomous grasping.
