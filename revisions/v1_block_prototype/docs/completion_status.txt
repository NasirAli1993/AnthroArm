Important modeling decisions
The source called its hand20DoF but supplied19 finger joints. This implementation adds a fourth thumb axis to provide20 finger axes plus5 arm axes.
The source examples included a50mm cube; the reproducible seeded grasp demonstration uses a36mm cube at60g. Its dimensions/mass are in robot.xml and generate_model.py.
The detailed CAD and simplified physics geometry share joint frames and major dimensions. CAD density/materials are not used to derive the assumed physics masses. Valid CAD solids do not establish manufacturability.
Position-only IK does not plan collision-free paths or control arbitrary hand orientation. The dashboard distinguishes predicted kinematic solution error from the measured position error under gravitational load.
