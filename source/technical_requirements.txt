TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS AND VERIFIED PROJECT SCOPE

This neutral engineering brief describes the project requirements and current
software deliverables. The original conversational input and supplied reference
images remain in the local backup; they are not redistributed in this export.

1. Model a recognizably humanoid arm and articulated five-finger hand, with
   smooth anatomical mechanical surfaces rather than block-only finger shapes.
2. Preserve editable native FreeCAD models, STEP/STL exports, joint definitions,
   component explanations, scripts, and visible design/simulation evidence.
3. Provide seven arm axes and twenty hand axes with kinematics, bounded dynamic
   actuation, collision/contact modeling, and measured simulation checks.
4. Demonstrate actual contact pickup, lift, hold, set-down, and release of a free
   simulated object without attaching or teleporting it during the task.
5. Use calibrated virtual RGB-D image/depth observations for target location.
   Separate measured performance from ground-truth evaluation data.
6. Fit a real learned component and evaluate held-out conditions. The delivered
   component is a small size-conditioned regression with scripted task stages
   and numerical IK, restricted to upright cubes, not a general VLA policy.
7. Demonstrate sensor-based mobile navigation and a continuous mounted-arm
   docking/pickup experiment. The standalone obstacle benchmark and heavy-base
   clear-lane combined experiment are distinct validated scopes.
8. Provide actual ROS 2 interfaces, reproducible tests, logs of quantitative
   results, recorded demonstrations, and documented failures and limitations.
9. Preserve earlier model revisions and mechanical/SOLIDWORKS preparation work.
   The tiny native-named SOLIDWORKS test fixtures are not a recovered usable
   SOLIDWORKS assembly; FreeCAD and neutral exports are the usable CAD assets.
10. Keep the implemented environment and project data on D:. The documented
    tested reproduction root is D:\RoboticArm. Public source includes pinned
    dependencies and model provenance; installed runtimes/caches and the large
    downloadable model weight are not bundled in ordinary Git.
11. The current requested outcome is software/simulation only. No physical
    robot or Jetson is required. Isaac Sim/Lab was not installed or executed on
    the unsupported local GPU; its feasibility and remaining work are stated.

See README.md, docs/SETUP.md, the validation reports, and the offline simulation
presentation for exact measurements, installation details, and scope limits.
