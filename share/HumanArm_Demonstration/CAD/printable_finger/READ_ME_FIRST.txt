SINGLE TENDON FINGER: FIT-TEST PROTOTYPE

Units are millimetres. These are separate mechanical test parts, not the visual full-arm shells.

1. First print one proximal/middle hinge fit coupon or the two parts. Use an M2 smooth-shank reference pin. Clear/ream the 2.3 mm bores only as needed. The 5.2 mm tongue has 0.4 mm nominal clearance per side in the 6 mm clevis.
2. Dry-fit the full finger manually. Check that all three joints move freely, the rear stop tabs arrest hyperextension, and no pin/screw head rubs a neighbor. Actual stop angle is unvalidated.
3. Thread a flexible 0.5–0.8 mm cord through the 1.4 mm flexor channels and distal transverse anchor; keep the path smooth across each hinge. Add a lightly tensioned external elastic return through the dorsal anchor holes.
4. Measure the actual servo, supplied horn, flange span and output offset. The cradle is generic and slotted. The 40.8 x 20.0 x 37.8 mm Hitec body envelope is verified from its official drawing; complete mount compatibility is NOT verified.
5. Attach the 7 mm-radius drum to an actual compatible purchased horn. No printed spline is supplied. Drill/match horn fastening as required; keep screws clear of the servo case.
6. Clamp the base. Test by pulling the tendon by hand before attaching or powering a motor. Use the separate prototype electrical guide and conservative firmware only after manual travel/return tests pass.

This design has not been printed, assembled, load-tested or powered. Additional shims, retained fasteners, supports and fit adjustments may be required.
